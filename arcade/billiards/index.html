How to play

1Aim: drag anywhere on the table to swing the aim line around the cue ball. Tap a ball to aim straight at it.
2Fine aim: slide the ridged wheel on the right for tiny adjustments.
3Shoot: pull the cue on the left down and let go. Push it back up to cancel.
4Spin: tap the cue ball button to hit with follow, draw or side.
5Rules: pot your group (solids 1–7 or stripes 9–15), then the 8. Fouls give your opponent ball in hand: drag the cue ball anywhere.
