💀 OG Blaster

Classic OG build: no more blocky cubes. Dark tech-base and hell-brick corridors, smooth detailed wall textures, and real monsters — soldiers, horned imps, pink brutes, and floating eye-orbs — each with its own shape, not just a colored box.

Desktop: WASD / arrows to move · mouse to look (click game to lock mouse) · click or Space to shoot · Shift to run
Mobile: left joystick to move · drag right side to look · FIRE button

Clear each wave. You heal +25 and get ammo between waves. Don’t let them touch you!

OG build: 4 different monsters — soldier (balanced), horned imp (fast), pink brute (tanky, big mouth), floating eye-orb (round, flies at face height). Wave 1 spawns right in front of you, monsters pathfind through the maze to reach you, every monster has a bright outline + name + health bar, and the minimap (top-right) shows pale walls, yellow you, and colored monster dots.
