🧱 Blocky Blaster V2

Depth test version: strong distance fog, dark wall seams, north/south wall shading, shadowed wall bottoms, and a perspective checker floor — so the wall right in front of you pops from the same-color wall far away.

Desktop: WASD / arrows to move · mouse to look (click game to lock mouse) · click or Space to shoot · Shift to run
Mobile: left joystick to move · drag right side to look · FIRE button

Clear each wave. You heal +25 and get ammo between waves. Don’t let them touch you!

V2 depth changes: far walls get dark + blue-fogged, side-facing walls are half as bright as front-facing ones, every block has a black seam and its own tint, wall bottoms are shadowed, and the floor is a real perspective checkerboard (grass vs tan dirt) so it never blends with the walls.
