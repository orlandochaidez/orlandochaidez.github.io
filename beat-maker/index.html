How to cook: tap the pads to jam, or hit LOAD PRESET then PLAY for an instant beat. Build your own in the step sequencer, flip kits mid-loop (your pattern stays, the sounds change), and use REC to record pad playing into the loop. Keyboard works too: 1234 top row of pads, QWER, ASDF, ZXCV bottom row. Space = play/stop. The SAMPLES bank holds real audio bites: pad 13 is the Strum, pad 14 is a guitar loop — tap it once to start it looping, tap again to stop it. Want a pad in the sequencer? Hit + ADD ROW, then tap any pad — it gets its own row (up to 8 extra). Your rows and patterns save on this device automatically.
