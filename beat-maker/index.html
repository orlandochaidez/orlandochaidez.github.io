How to cook: tap the pads to jam, or hit LOAD PRESET then PLAY for an instant beat. Build your own in the step sequencer, flip kits mid-loop (your pattern stays, the sounds change), and use REC to record pad playing into the loop. Keyboard works too: 1234 top row of pads, QWER, ASDF, ZXCV bottom row. Space = play/stop. The SAMPLES bank holds real audio bites: pad 13 is the Strum, pad 14 is a guitar loop — tap it once to start it looping, tap again to stop it. Want a pad in the sequencer? Hit + ADD ROW, then tap any pad — it gets its own row (up to 8 extra). Your rows and patterns save on this device automatically. The ★ MY KIT bank is yours to build: hit ASSIGN PADS, tap the pad you want to change, switch to any kit, and tap the sound you want on it — the Eagles loop next to Boom Bap drums, whatever you want. Your bank saves on this device too. The Hotel Loop is BPM-locked — it stretches to whatever tempo you set, and in the sequencer a step only ever starts it, never stops it. TAP TEMPO: tap the button in time with a beat in your head and the tempo follows.
